Methods and code

How do individual uptake profiles and plant-level mean uptake vary across population and treatment, without assuming an undocumented matched-pair crosswalk or conducting small-sample inference?

Sources Sf2ebd0a69642 S7528fd2c1ece S0aadb060bb3b
Assumptions
  • The installed datasets::CO2 object is the accessible quantitative input. Its software version, units attribute, and structure are printed; it is not claimed to be a verified transcription of every value in the paper’s OCR table.
  • Each plant was measured at seven CO₂ concentrations. Three plants were measured for each population-by-chilling-treatment combination. The code validates these features.
  • Repeated observations are summarized within plants before describing between-group differences. Pointwise standard deviations describe dispersion across plants at a concentration, not independent replication across concentration levels.
  • The source describes random pairing of control plants with plants designated for chilling, but no explicit crosswalk from the paper’s numbered plants to R’s Plant identifiers is supplied. Similar suffixes are not evidence of pairing; paired calculations are omitted.
  • Group-mean contrasts are descriptive only. No hypothesis tests, confidence intervals, or small-sample inferential claims are produced.
  • Each plant received the seven CO₂ levels in increasing, consecutive order. Curve shape cannot separate concentration effects from possible sequence effects.
  • The inspected R help page defines uptake as carbon dioxide uptake rate in micromoles per square metre per second. This documentation-based interpretation is distinct from the units attribute printed directly from the installed object.
  • Concentration values are preserved exactly as stored. Observed metadata are not treated as external confirmation of concentration units; conflicting labels or units are not silently reconciled.
  • The plant mean equally weights the measured concentration levels. It is not an integral over concentration, time-integrated carbon gain, or fitted physiological capacity.
  • The source-supported sequence is 14 hours of daytime chilling at 7°C, then 10 hours of recovery at 20°C, then measurement at 26°C. R’s documentation instead describes overnight chilling. This conflict is retained; profiles are not described as direct measurements during chilling.
  • All generated files use accepted CSV or PNG formats. The two graphics devices are closed separately, with no graphics-parameter restoration after device closure. Only base R and bundled packages are used, without installation, network, environment-variable, or credential access.
